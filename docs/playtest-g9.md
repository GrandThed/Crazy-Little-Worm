# G-9 Fun-check playtest — note sheet

**Protocol:** 3 full solo days at real speed (no debug time scale — the 12-minute
pacing is what's being tested). Debug panel allowed only for reading stats, not
for changing them. Day 1 is anomaly-free by design; days 2–3 each have a ~70%
chance of one infected at a random time. When done, the findings below get
distilled into a go/no-go section in [game-design.md](game-design.md) and G-9
closes — and M1 with it.

**What the design doc predicts** (§8 sanity numbers, worth checking against the
Summary screen): ~55 arrivals over a day at ~4.6/min · income ~450–550 at fee 10
· one stand at player speed (~8 inspections/min) comfortably beats the arrival
rate, so queue pressure should stay low but not zero.

## The questions that decide go/no-go

1. **Is inspection fun at minute 10, or only at minute 1?** (The stamp loop is
   the whole game — if it's a chore by day 2, M2 content won't save it.)
2. **Did you ever feel the three-way tension** (wave-through vs. scrutiny vs.
   deny-all), or does one strategy obviously dominate? Which one?
3. **Was the infected legible?** Did you spot a tell and feel clever, or did it
   feel random whether one slipped in?
4. **Was the outbreak a drop-everything moment or an annoyance?**
5. **12-minute day: too long, too short, or right?** Where in the day did your
   attention dip?
6. **Did you ever look at the cash number and make a decision because of it?**
   (If no — the economy isn't in the loop yet, which is fine to note.)
7. **Anything you did just because it was fun** (not optimal)? That's signal.
